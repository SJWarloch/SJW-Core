Principles

Access is a foundational axiom,
not a marketing gimmick.

"Free" means actually usable

Digital products are designed around permanent and meaningful free access, not a deliberately restrictive and predatory demo tier.

Time is valuable

ALL premium content should have legitimate earn-through-engagement acquisition paths.

Physical games stay printable

Tabletop products always keep print-and-play access available even when premium editions exist.

No hostage-taking

SJW is built so accounts, credits, entitlements, and ownership can survive individual products, and even the company itself.

Subversive Joy Works: Credits

One account, endless opportunities

Your SJW account links together all individual Subversive Joy Works products with a universal non-purchasable currency: Credits. Earn Credits by engaging with any SJW game or app and they're stored here, ready to be spent anywhere. Individual products may have their own supplemental premium currencies as required by their respective distribution platforms, but Credits will always be directly substitutable across the entire ecosystem.
